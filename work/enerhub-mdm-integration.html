Case study · US Oil and Gas Operator

EnerHub MDM integration

A commercial MDM covering only part of the estate.

Problem

A commercial MDM covering only part of the estate.

Approach

Developed the end-to-end integration of the EnerHub cross-reference system across a US operator's Snowflake environment. Prepared Well Master reporting views and - critically - built an additional architecture and development layer over EnerHub to incorporate resources the commercial product was missing. Extended the implementation to support broader Snowflake integration, master-data alignment, and reporting requirements across the estate.

Architecture

When the MDM product isn't enough

Commercial MDM tools cover the standard cases; real estates have the other cases. The extension layer built here didn't replace EnerHub - it completed it, adding the missing resources while keeping the product as the system of record. Extend, don't fork.

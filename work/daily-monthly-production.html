Case study · US Oil and Gas Operator

Daily & monthly production reporting

Daily ops numbers and monthly accounting numbers that never agreed.

Problem

Daily ops numbers and monthly accounting numbers that never agreed.

Approach

Developed the daily and monthly production reporting suite for a US operator based on the ProCount production accounting system - the operational heartbeat reports that production and management teams open every morning and every close. Built on the governed Snowflake layer so daily operational numbers and monthly accounting numbers reconcile by construction rather than by month-end firefighting.

Architecture

Daily and monthly must agree

The oldest fight in production reporting: the daily number ops saw versus the monthly number accounting booked. Building both from the same governed foundation, with allocation logic applied consistently, retires that fight - one lineage, two cadences, zero surprises.

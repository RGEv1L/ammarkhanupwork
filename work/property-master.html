Property Master sub-system

Problem

Every system answering “which property is this?” differently.

Approach

Developed and co-designed the Property Master sub-system on Snowflake for a US energy operator, providing a single unified view of properties across the organization. Co-maintained the subsystem post-delivery, supporting its reliability and enhancement as part of the broader Snowflake reporting architecture. The result: a centralized property-data foundation giving every downstream report a consistent answer to "which property is this?"

Architecture

Property is where money meets land

Properties connect ownership, accounting, and operations - and every system defines them differently. A governed Property Master means revenue, cost, and land reports finally aggregate on the same entities. Boring name, foundational impact.
